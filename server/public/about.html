About Sizlio

Built in Pakistan, for Pakistani restaurants.

Sizlio is a complete restaurant management platform built for how restaurants in Pakistan actually operate — unreliable internet, busy counters, delivery demand, and teams that need every screen in sync.

We started after watching owners juggle separate tools for billing, kitchen, riders, and stock — with orders getting lost between them. Goal was simple: one connected system that keeps working when the internet doesn’t.

What we believe

Offline-first, always

Counter should never stop taking orders because the net dropped. Sync happens automatically when connection returns.

One system, not six

Counter, Kitchen (Serve-tak), Waiter, Rider, QR Ordering, Inventory, and FBR should talk to each other — not live in separate apps.

Built with real restaurants

Features come from cafés, desi restaurants, and fast-food teams across Pakistan — offline resilience, recipe stock, and full order status till serve.

Where we’re headed

We’re expanding multi-branch support, deeper reporting, and more integrations — always on the same offline-resilient foundation.
