How can we help?

Common questions from restaurant owners using Sizlio.

Does Counter really work without internet?

Yes. Orders, bills, and discounts continue offline and save locally. Everything auto-syncs to Kitchen, Waiter, Rider, and Inventory when connection returns — including offline receipt printing.

What is “Serve-tak” on Kitchen Display?

KDS tracks full status: Pending → Accepted → Preparing → Ready → Served → Completed. Delivery orders also support Ready to deliver → Out for delivery → Delivered.

How does QR table ordering work?

Each table gets a unique QR. Customers scan with their phone camera, browse the live menu, place an order, or call the waiter — no app download.

How does kitchen inventory auto-deduct?

Link ingredients to menu items (recipes). Stock is checked when the order is placed and automatically deducted when the order is served — not at order time.

Is FBR supported?

Yes. Enable FBR in settings, add your credentials, and invoices can be submitted with FBR QR on the receipt. Failed submissions can be retried. You remain responsible for correct tax configuration.

What happens when I switch plans?

All plans include every feature. Plans only differ in how many staff accounts you can create per role (Waiter, Counter, Kitchen, Rider, etc.). Upgrading raises those limits.

Multi-branch / custom limits?

Yes — message sales on WhatsApp for custom staff limits and multi-branch pricing.
