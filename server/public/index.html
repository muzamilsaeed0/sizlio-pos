📋 How It Works

From order to serving — smooth flow

Every step connected, every screen in sync.

1

Order Received

Customer orders via QR, waiter, counter, or phone. All channels merge into one queue.

2

Kitchen Prepares

Chef accepts on Kitchen Display, sets prep time, tickets auto-print. Inventory checks ingredient availability.

3

Serve or Deliver

Waiter gets ready notification. Delivery orders auto-assign to the least-loaded rider on shift.

4

Complete & Track

Payment collected, inventory auto-deducted via recipes, shift reports generated automatically.
