Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information we collect

When you use Sizlio, we collect information you provide (restaurant name, contact details, staff accounts) and operational data generated through the platform — orders, inventory transactions, sales records, shift reports, and FBR-related invoice logs where enabled.

2. How we use your data

  • To operate Counter POS, Kitchen Display (KDS), Waiter App, Rider App, QR Ordering, Inventory, and Status Board
  • To generate sales dashboards, shift reports, stock alerts, and recipe-based deductions
  • To process FBR submissions when you enable tax compliance (invoice number, QR, retry logs)
  • To provide support and keep data synced across your devices

3. Data storage & security

Data is stored on secured servers with encryption in transit. Offline Counter orders are saved locally on your device (IndexedDB) until sync, and are protected by the same account access controls. We do not sell your restaurant data.

4. Sharing of information

Information is shared only with service providers needed to run the platform (hosting, notifications, FBR gateway where applicable) under confidentiality obligations, or when required by law.

5. Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, corrections, or account deletion at any time by emailing sales@sizlio.com.

6. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated to account owners in advance.
