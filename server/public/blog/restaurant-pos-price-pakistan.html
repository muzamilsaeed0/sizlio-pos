Restaurant POS Price in Pakistan (2026): Software, Hardware & Hidden Costs

Updated November 2026 · 8 min read · Sizlio

“POS kitne ka hai?” ka seedha jawab market me nahi milta — kyunki software fee, printer, setup, FBR module aur per-terminal charges alag-alag vendors pe alag hote hain. Yeh guide budget banane me madad karti hai.

1) Software (monthly / yearly)

Pakistan me published restaurant POS plans aksar chand hazaar se ~10,000+ PKR / month / branch ke band me dikhte hain (vendor aur features pe depend). “Free POS” models me kabhi card commission ya limited features hoti hain — contract padho.

Pucho: kitne terminals included? Kitchen / rider screens extra? Support WhatsApp pe? Trial kitne din?

2) Hardware (one-time)

  • Counter device — tablet ya used PC
  • 80mm thermal printer — receipts / KOT
  • Kitchen display — optional tablet/monitor (KDS guide)
  • Cash drawer, optional

Cloud POS me mehnga proprietary terminal zaroori nahi — browser + reliable device kaafi ho sakta hai.

3) Setup & training

Kuch vendors setup free rakhte hain; kuch per-branch fee lete hain. Menu entry, staff training, aur printer config time leti hai — pehle se pooch lo kaun karega.

4) Tax / FBR related cost

FBR digital invoicing requirements business type aur turnover pe depend karti hain. Kuch POS me module included hota hai, kuch me add-on monthly charge. Details: FBR POS restaurant Pakistan.

5) Hidden costs checklist

  • Per-terminal / per-user fees
  • Mandatory annual maintenance (AMC)
  • Payment device tied to high MDR
  • Online ordering commission “bundled”
  • Data export / lock-in

Cafe vs full restaurant budget

Small cafe: single counter + printer — Cafe Lite style kam screens, kam training.

Full restaurant: counter + kitchen KDS + optional waiter/rider — zyada devices, thoda zyada software tier.
