Restaurant Inventory POS Pakistan: Recipes, Not Only Spreadsheets

Updated November 2026 · 7 min read · Sizlio

Hafte ke end pe stock count aur “chicken kitna gaya” ka andaza — food cost control nahi. Restaurant inventory tab strong hota hai jab menu recipes stock se linked hon.

Simple vs recipe inventory

  • Simple: finished goods count (bottles, packs)
  • Recipe: burger sell → bun + patty + sauce deduct
Serve pe deduct: kitchen jab order serve mark kare tab ingredients kam hon — pehle se rough guess kam.

Pakistan kitchens me common gaps

  • Purchase register alag, sales alag
  • Wastage undocumented
  • Store room se kitchen transfer oral
  • High-cost items (meat, cheese) untracked

POS inventory modules worth having

  • Ingredients + units (kg, liter, pcs)
  • Recipes per menu item / size
  • Supplier / purchase entry
  • Kitchen stock request / transfer
  • Low stock visibility
