POS software kya hota hai?

Point of Sale (POS) software wahan use hota hai jahan customer pay karta hai — order entry, bill, payment, aur aksar kitchen/stock reports. Retail barcode POS aur restaurant POS alag needs rakhte hain.

“Best” decide karne ki 8 tests

  1. Offline bill — net / load-shedding pe order rukta hai ya nahi? (Offline POS)
  2. Speed — 10 items + discount + print kitne taps me?
  3. Order types — dine-in, takeaway, delivery clear hain?
  4. Kitchen path — KOT/KDS ya paper chaos? (KDS)
  5. Staff roles — manager, counter, kitchen alag login?
  6. Reports — aaj ki sales, shifts, refunds
  7. Support — WhatsApp pe Pakistan hours?
  8. Total cost — software + printer + hidden fees (POS price)
Tip: Vendor ko apna real menu dekar 15-minute live demo mang — screenshots se decision mat lo.

Restaurant vs general “POS Pakistan”

Bohot listings general retail POS mix karti hain. Agar aap cafe/restaurant ho to table, modifiers, deals, rider assign zaroori ho sakte hain — dekho Restaurant POS Pakistan aur Cafe POS.

FBR / tax angle

Growing businesses ko invoice compliance sochna padta hai. Module included hai ya add-on? Guide: FBR POS.
