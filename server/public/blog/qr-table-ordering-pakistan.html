QR Table Ordering Pakistan: Menu on Phone, Orders in Your POS

Updated November 2026 · 7 min read · Sizlio

Dine-in peak pe waiter har table pe pehla order lene me late ho jata hai. QR table ordering se guest apna phone scan karke menu kholta hai aur order bhejta hai — customer app install ki zaroorat nahi.

Kaise kaam karta hai

  • Har table ka unique QR
  • Scan → browser menu
  • Items add → order place (ya waiter call)
  • Order counter / kitchen queue me aata hai
Important: Guest side internet (mobile data/Wi‑Fi) chahiye. Staff POS alag se offline mode support kar sakta hai — Offline POS.

Pakistan restaurants ke liye faida

  • Pehli order entry tez
  • Less “galat item” jab guest khud select kare
  • Waiter load kam — service pe focus
  • Paper menu print/update cost kam

Setup checklist

  • Tables numbered + laminated QR stickers
  • Menu prices POS me up-to-date
  • Kitchen/counter notifications on
  • Staff training: QR order kaise accept/print
