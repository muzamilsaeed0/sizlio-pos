Cafe POS Pakistan: Single-Counter Billing for Small Cafes

Updated October 2026 · 7 min read · Sizlio

Chhota cafe, coffee shop, ya juice corner — yahan full restaurant jaisa waiter + kitchen screen setup aksar overkill hota hai. Phir bhi notebook se bill banana slow aur error-prone hai. Is gap ko cafe POS Pakistan bharata hai: ek counter, tez menu, takeaway/delivery, payment, aur daily sales. Sizlio Cafe Lite isi use-case ke liye banaya gaya hai.

Cafe ko restaurant POS se farq kyun chahiye?

Restaurant POS tables, courses, KDS, aur multi-role staff ke liye hota hai. Cafe mein aksar:

  • 1 counter pe order + payment
  • Kam staff (1–2 log)
  • Tez takeaway line
  • Kabhi delivery rider
  • Simple menu + deals (combo, discount)

Agar system mein 6 screens aur complex login hon, training lambi aur galtiyan zyada. Is liye cafe POS software simple hona chahiye.

Cafe POS Pakistan — must-have features

  1. Fast item add — categories, search, deals
  2. Order types — dine-in, takeaway, delivery
  3. Pay now / pay later — cafe flow ke mutabiq
  4. Thermal print — customer bill
  5. Offline counter — net drop pe bhi sale ( offline POS guide)
  6. Daily sales — shift close pe clear number
  7. Optional rider — jab delivery shuru karo

Sizlio Cafe Lite kya hai?

Cafe Lite Sizlio ka single-screen plan/mode hai chhoti shops ke liye:

  • Ek counter screen se order → payment → complete
  • Dine-in / takeaway / delivery type select
  • Table number optional (full restaurant jaisa force nahi)
  • Delivery pe rider assign + COD flow
  • Restaurant-wise local order numbers (#1, #2…)
  • Jab business barhe — full kitchen / waiter screens same platform pe
Idea: Aaj cafe, kal full floor — software dobara na kharido. Lite se start, growth pe modules on.

Hardware — cafe ke liye practical setup

  • 1 tablet ya small PC (counter)
  • Thermal receipt printer
  • Optional: cash drawer, second display
  • Stable power (UPS) — load-shedding ke liye

Mehengi multi-terminal kit tab lo jab genuinely multi-station darkar ho.

Common cafe mistakes

  • Full restaurant POS lena jab sirf counter darkar ho
  • Offline mode ignore karna (rush + net drop = chaos)
  • Menu / deals POS pe update na karna — staff memory pe depend
  • Delivery WhatsApp pe alag, billing alag — numbers match nahi hote

Cafe vs full restaurant — kab upgrade?

Upgrade socho jab:

  • Alag kitchen team ho aur tickets miss ho rahe hon
  • Waiters table service le rahe hon
  • Multi-branch / zyada concurrent orders
