POS system kya hai?

POS = Point of Sale — woh point jahan sale complete hoti hai. System me usually:

  • Software (menu, bill, reports)
  • Screen (PC / tablet)
  • Receipt printer
  • Optional: cash drawer, scanner, kitchen display

Pakistan me log POS kyun lete hain

  • Notebook se tez billing
  • Daily sales clear
  • Staff mistakes kam
  • Tax / invoice readiness jab scale ho
  • Kitchen aur delivery orders ek jagah

Retail POS vs restaurant POS

Retail: barcode, SKU stock, quick scan.
Restaurant: modifiers, tables, KOT/KDS, delivery, deals.

Galat type choose karne se kitchen flow toot jata hai. Restaurant focus: Restaurant POS Pakistan.

“POS machine” zaroori hai?

Purane sense me dedicated terminal. Aaj bohot businesses tablet + thermal printer + cloud/software se kaam chala rahe hain. Cost guide: POS price Pakistan.

Pakistan tip: Sirf online POS mat lo — offline billing poocho. Offline POS · Cloud vs desktop.
