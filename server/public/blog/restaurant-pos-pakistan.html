Restaurant POS Pakistan 2026: Offline Billing, FBR & Cafe POS Guide

Updated October 2026 · 8 min read · Sizlio

Agar aap Pakistan mein restaurant, cafe, ya fast-food chala rahe hain, to sahi restaurant POS sirf bill print nahi karta — woh kitchen, delivery, stock aur tax compliance ko ek system mein jorta hai. Yeh guide batati hai ke 2026 mein Pakistani restaurants ko POS se kya chahiye, aur Sizlio is market ke liye kaise design hua hai.

Restaurant POS Pakistan mein kyun zaroori hai?

Pehle kaam copy-register aur WhatsApp orders se chal jata tha. Aaj customers QR, card, aur fast service expect karte hain. Owner ko chahiye:

  • Har order ka clear record (dine-in, takeaway, delivery)
  • Kitchen tak ghalat order na jaye
  • Shift ke end pe sales ka number ready ho
  • Load-shedding / weak internet pe bhi counter na rukey
  • Jahan darkar ho, FBR-style digital invoice support

Is liye POS system Pakistan ab retail-only tool nahi — restaurants ke liye alag workflow chahiye: tables, KOT/KDS, riders, deals.

Offline POS Pakistan — load-shedding reality

Bohot se cloud POS tab ruk jate hain jab internet drop ho. Pakistani cities mein yeh roz ka masla ho sakta hai. Ek strong offline restaurant POS counter pe order lena, bill print karna, aur baad mein sync karna allow karta hai.

Sizlio approach: Counter offline kaam karta hai taake internet jane se sale na rukey. Sync connection wapas aane par hoti hai — yeh un restaurants ke liye critical hai jahan ups/generator ke bawajood line unstable ho.

FBR aur tax-ready billing

Pakistan mein kai businesses ko digital invoicing aur tax authority rules follow karne padte hain (FBR / provincial boards situation city aur business type se differ karti hai). Restaurant POS choose karte waqt poochhein:

  • Invoice pe business details clear hain?
  • FBR-style submission / QR support available hai ya roadmap pe?
  • Reports se daily sales nikalna asaan hai?

Sizlio ko Pakistani restaurants ke liye FBR-oriented compliance features ke sath design kiya gaya hai — taake billing aur documentation ek flow mein rahein.

Full restaurant vs Cafe Lite POS

Full restaurant POS

Jab waiter, kitchen, aur multi-station chahiye:

  • Counter + waiter ordering
  • Kitchen display till Serve
  • Delivery rider assign
  • Recipe inventory + suppliers
  • Manager reports

Cafe Lite — chhoti shops ke liye

Kai cafes ko full kitchen screen ki zaroorat nahi. Unhein ek counter screen chahiye: menu, order type (dine-in / takeaway / delivery), payment, aur complete. Sizlio Cafe Lite isi liye hai — single-screen POS, kam staff, tez training.

Features jo 2026 restaurant POS mein dekhni chahiye

  1. Order types — dine-in, takeaway, delivery
  2. KDS / serve status — kitchen se table tak visibility
  3. Offline counter — internet drop pe bhi sale
  4. Inventory & recipes — stock control, wastage kam
  5. Supplier ledger — purchases aur payments
  6. Rider flow — delivery assign aur COD
  7. Local order numbers — har branch apna #1, #2…
  8. Reports — daily sales, top items, payments
