Delivery Rider POS Pakistan: Apne Orders, Apna Rider, Ek System

Updated November 2026 · 7 min read · Sizlio

WhatsApp aur phone se delivery orders aate hain, lekin assign notebook pe hota hai — kaunsa rider gaya, COD kitna lena hai, order delivered hua ya nahi. Delivery-ready POS yeh chain digital karta hai.

Typical in-house delivery flow

  1. Counter pe order type = Delivery
  2. Address / phone note
  3. Kitchen ready
  4. Rider assign (auto ya manual)
  5. Rider delivered mark
  6. COD / payment settle → order complete
Cafe Lite: chhote cafes ek screen se order + rider assign kar sakte hain — Cafe POS Pakistan.

Aggregators vs apna delivery

Foodpanda/other platforms discovery dete hain lekin commission lagti hai. Direct orders pe margin behtar — is liye rider screen POS ke andar hona useful hai.

Rider POS me kya chahiye

  • Pending delivery list
  • Assign / accept order
  • Customer phone / area visible
  • Mark delivered
  • COD amount clear
